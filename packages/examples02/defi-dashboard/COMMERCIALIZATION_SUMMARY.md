## 💡 Monetization Pillars Implemented

### Pillar A: Agent-as-a-Service (AaaS)
- **Revenue**: $47.5K MRR in demo
- **Model**: Subscription tiers + usage overages
- **Target**: dApp teams, DeFi protocols, DAOs
- **Why it converts**: Teams don't want to manage infra

### Pillar B: Transaction & Automation Fees
- **Revenue**: $8.4K/month in demo
- **Model**: 5-30 bps per automated transaction
- **Target**: dApps and merchants
- **Why it converts**: No upfront cost, aligned incentives

### Pillar C: Premium SDK & Enterprise Licenses
- **Revenue**: $30K/month in demo (2 enterprise customers)
- **Model**: $10K-$100K+ annual licenses
- **Target**: Funded teams, enterprises
- **Why it converts**: Legal clarity, SLA, support

### Pillar D: Merchant AI Ops Copilot
- **Revenue**: Separate product line
- **Model**: $99-299/month per merchant
- **Target**: Merchants, PSPs
- **Why it converts**: Merchants already pay for dashboards

### Pillar E: White-Label & Partnerships
- **Revenue**: Revenue share model
- **Model**: 5-20% + minimum guarantees
- **Target**: Wallets, PSPs, L2s
- **Why it converts**: Speed to market
